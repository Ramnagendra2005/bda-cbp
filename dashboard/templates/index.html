How it works

The data flows through a batch pipeline: raw files in, clean tables and answers out.

  1. Raw dataTwo CSV files: telecom customers and audit records.
  2. CleanSpark trims text, removes duplicates and fills blank charges for brand-new customers.
  3. EnrichAdds tenure groups, a churn flag, and risk, fraud and quality ratios.
  4. AnalyseSpark SQL groups and averages the data to answer each question.
  5. StoreResults are saved as Parquet, a fast, compressed column format.
  6. VisualiseThis dashboard reads the results and explains them.

Glossary

Every term on this page, in plain words.

Churn
A customer cancelling their service and leaving the company.
Churn rate
The percentage of customers in a group who left. 30% means 3 out of every 10.
Tenure
How long someone has been a customer, in months.
Contract
Month-to-month (cancel anytime), one-year or two-year commitment.
Add-on service
Optional extras such as online security, tech support, backup or device protection.
Revenue lost per month
The combined monthly bills of the customers who left.
Audit engagement
One audit job carried out by a firm for a client.
High-risk share
The percentage of audits flagged as high-risk.
Fraud cases
Audits in which fraud was detected.
Quality score
The average of audit effectiveness and client satisfaction, out of 10.
Revenue impact
The financial effect of the problems the audits found, in millions of US dollars.
Apache Spark
A Big Data engine that processes large datasets in parallel across many CPU cores or machines.
